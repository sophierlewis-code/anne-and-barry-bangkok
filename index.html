Choose your days

There are three day trips: the canal boat tour & Wat Arun, Chatuchak Weekend Market and the Damnoen Saduak Floating Market. You have two days, so pick one for each. Chatuchak is only open at weekends, so it can only go on Sunday.

The day trip options

Canal boat tour & Wat Arun

Sunday or Monday

Take a taxi to Poomjai Garden for 9:30, where Sophie meets you. The canal boat tour starts at 10:00, with a 30-minute stop at the Artist's House, and ends at Wat Arun around 12:00.

Lunch at Supanniga Eating Room at 13:30. We'll get taxis together during the day.

Once you've confirmed, Sophie can help you book.

Chatuchak Weekend Market

Sunday only (weekends only)

Sophie drives you from the hotel at 9:00. Allow 1–3 hours at Chatuchak, then lunch at Black Canyon Cafe near the market at 12:00.

Even with Jim Thompson House you're back by 15:30, so the early 19:30 cabaret show works. It's still a busy day with no wiggle room, so a river cruise isn't an option if you add Jim Thompson House.

Choosing Chatuchak means leaving out either the canal tour or the floating market.

Damnoen Saduak Floating Market

Sunday or Monday

Sophie joins you for the day and drives you from the hotel at 8:00. It's a 1½–2 hour drive, arriving around 10:00. Rowing-boat or motorboat tours run from 30 minutes to 2 hours and can be booked a couple of days ahead.

Lunch in or near the market, back at the hotel by 15:00–16:00. This is the best day for a river cruise in the evening.

Once you've confirmed, Sophie can help you book.

Jim Thompson House

Afternoon add-on after the canal tour or Chatuchak

After Chatuchak: taxi at 13:00, arrive at Jim Thompson House at 13:30 and spend about 90 minutes there, back at the hotel around 15:30.

After the canal tour: taxi at 15:00 for the 45-minute guided tour at 15:30, back at the hotel around 17:00.

It fits more easily after Chatuchak. Either way it makes for a busy day with no wiggle room, so a river cruise won't fit that evening. After the canal tour, the later 21:00 cabaret show is best.

The evening options

Fitting the evenings around the day trips River cruise – The cruise fits well after the floating market, as you're back by 15:00–16:00. It also works after the canal tour or Chatuchak if you're not doing Jim Thompson House. Adding Jim Thompson House makes it a busy day with no wiggle room, so the cruise isn't an option then. Cabaret show – If you're doing the canal tour and Jim Thompson House, it will be a busy day, so I'd recommend the later 21:00 show. After Chatuchak and Jim Thompson House it's still a busy day, but you're back by 15:30, so the earlier 19:30 show works. On any other day, either show works. Asiatique dinner only – Works any night, at any time you like. Sky bar – Works any night, at any time you like. Please note – Sophie won't join you for the evening plans, apart from Saturday. She will join you during the day and help you book venues and get taxis for your evening plans.

Asiatique & Calypso Cabaret

Any night

Asiatique is a riverside night market with lots of restaurants, 5–10 minutes by taxi from your hotel. Have dinner there, then the Calypso Cabaret, which is inside Asiatique.

Shows at 19:30 and 21:00. If you're doing the canal tour and Jim Thompson House that day, book the 21:00 show. After Chatuchak and Jim Thompson House, the 19:30 show works. On any other day, either show works.

If you'd rather skip the show, Asiatique is still an easy, accessible and nice place for dinner, with different choices of food.

Saffron Cruise, Banyan Tree

Best on your floating market day

The only dinner cruise I could find with proper vegetarian food. Set menu about £80, roughly £94 each with tax and service, before drinks. A good hotel group with good food, so worth it if it suits your budget.

Take the hotel shuttle boat at 18:00 to ICONSIAM; the cruise leaves at 18:30. Saffron Cruise · Hotel shuttle boat times
